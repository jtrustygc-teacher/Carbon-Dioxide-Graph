Lab Instructions: Reading CO2 Estimates

Plants adjust their density of stomata (pores) relative to atmospheric CO2 levels. This application maps the inverse relationship using paleoclimate calibration datasets.

  • Drag or Keyboard Slider: Move the slider along the curve to observe how CO2 concentrations decrease as Stomatal Index increases.
  • Data Capping: Estimates are strictly capped at 850 ppm CO2 (corresponding to 6.5% SI) because experimental data points do not extend further.
  • Check Lab Values: Type your calculated Stomatal Index percentage into the input box to directly read the estimated atmospheric CO2 concentration for your lab report.
