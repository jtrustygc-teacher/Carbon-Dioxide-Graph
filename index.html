Atmospheric CO₂ & Stomatal Index Estimator

Lab Goal & Instructions

Plants adjust their leaf stomatal density based on ambient carbon dioxide levels. Higher $CO_2$ causes plants to produce fewer stomata per unit area (lower Stomatal Index), creating an inverse relationship used by paleoclimatologists to reconstruct ancient Earth atmospheres.

Instructions: Use the sliders or numeric input fields below to move along the best-fit calibration curve. Observe how the estimated atmospheric $CO_2$ changes dynamically.
